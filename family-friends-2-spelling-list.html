Family and Friends 2 — Spelling Lists

16 units · 2 tests per unit · 5–8 words per test · curated for 2nd-grade ESL spellers

How these were chosen. Words are pulled from each unit's curriculum, then filtered for spelling value: single words only (phrases reduced to their core word), high-frequency and decodable, and matched to the grade level. Where it helps, Test 1 drills the unit's theme vocabulary and Test 2 drills the unit's phonics pattern (the most testable, rule-based words).
  • Dropped: multi-word compounds, low-frequency or hard-to-spell items for this level.
  • Phonics words double as spelling-rule practice (CVC, digraphs, blends, magic-e, long vowels).
