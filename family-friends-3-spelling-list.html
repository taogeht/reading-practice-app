Family and Friends 3 — Spelling Lists

16 units · 2 tests per unit · 5–8 words per test · curated for 3rd-grade ESL spellers

How these were chosen. Words are pulled from each unit's curriculum, then filtered for spelling value: single words only (phrases reduced to their core word), high-frequency and decodable, matched to the grade level. Generally Test 1 drills the unit's theme vocabulary and Test 2 drills the unit's phonics pattern — Book 3 teaches real spelling families (magic-e, r-controlled, long vowels, end blends), which make ideal, rule-based spelling words.
  • Where the vocabulary is especially useful (countries, food, verbs, adjectives), both tests can be vocabulary.
  • Dropped: multi-word compounds, abbreviations (TV, P.E.), and lower-value items for this level.
