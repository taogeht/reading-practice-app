Family and Friends 1 — Spelling Lists

16 units · 2 tests per unit · 5 words per test · curated for new grade 1 ESL learners

How these were chosen. Each test is exactly 5 words, picked for spelling value at the entry level: short, decodable, high-frequency, concrete.
  • Test 1 always uses the unit's easiest words. Test 2 uses the next tier — or, from Unit 8 on, the unit's phonics pattern (digraphs sh/ch, then short vowels: classic CVC words).
  • No word repeats across the whole book, so high-frequency words (e.g. red, in, run, teacher) are tested once, in the unit where they best fit.
  • Dropped: two-word items ("teddy bear", "ice cream", "police officer") and the hardest spellings where a unit had spare words. A few long words (e.g. firefighter, rectangle) remain in Test 2 because they are the unit's core vocabulary.
